About VibeVote

VibeVote is a high-fidelity polling platform designed to bridge the gap between social communities. We provide real-time battle polls with deep source analytics, allowing creators to see exactly where their audience's "vibe" is coming from.

How it Works

Using Cloudflare's edge network and D1 database, we track and visualize every vote instantly. Our unique source-detection technology identifies traffic from platforms like X, Reddit, and Naver without invasive tracking or complex UTM parameters.
